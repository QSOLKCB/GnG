THE SHORT VERSION

Mischief with a mission.

In each district, rescue 3 gremlins, recover 2 tech cores, and serve 2 actors. Then press E at the gate. The final district also has a Grand Auditor to stun into submission.

Move & jump
A / D or arrows. Space, W or ↑ to jump. Release early for a shorter hop.
Stun
Hold J or Z. Unlimited stun bolts. Actors require paperwork instead.
Rescue & receipts
Touch free gremlins and dropped tech. Stun a cage guard, then press E, K or X nearby. The same button serves a nearby actor; receipts are never wasted on empty air.
Second chances
Flags restore armour and set checkpoints. If you fall, retry with collected objectives intact. There are no limited lives or surprise second campaign.
Controller
Standard gamepad: stick / D-pad move, A jump, X stun, B / Y use, Start pause. Release controls after resuming. On-screen controls support simultaneous touches.
Take a breath
P / Esc pauses. Switching tabs pauses too. Sound is optional. Reduced motion follows your system preference.

Original code, pixel art, maps and synthesised audio. Inspired by the gothic arcade platformer tradition and QSOL's ARCADE. Fictional factions, very real paperwork.
